Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

This program will not transfer any information to other networked systems unless specifically requested by the user or the person installing or operating it.

What the app connects to

  • Your database servers and SSH hosts. Quantum SQL connects only to the servers you configure.
  • The optional AI assistant. Only when you ask it something, the app sends your question and the related schema (table and column names, never row data) to the AI provider you picked, using your own API key. That provider's privacy policy applies to what it receives.

What the app does not do

  • No telemetry, analytics, crash reporting or tracking.
  • No automatic update checks.
  • No accounts. The developer never receives your data.

Data stored on your computer

Saved connections, settings, query history and saved queries stay on your computer:

  • Windows: %APPDATA%\QuantumSQL\
  • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/QuantumSQL/
  • Linux: ~/.config/QuantumSQL/

Passwords and API keys are stored encrypted. Uninstalling the app and deleting that folder removes all of it.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and sets no cookies or analytics of its own. It loads fonts from Google Fonts. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.
